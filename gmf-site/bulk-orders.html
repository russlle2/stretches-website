A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you send it

Can I mix garments, colors and sizes?

Yes. Add a separate line for each garment and color, then enter the size quantities. Use the notes for any special requirements. Availability will be confirmed in your quote.

What if I don’t have finished artwork?

Select “Need design help” or “Will send later.” Include an inspiration link or describe your idea so the team can discuss the next steps.

What is the minimum order or turnaround time?

Minimums and production time depend on the garment and decoration. Submit your quantity and requested date so the team can confirm what’s possible.

When do I pay?

No payment is collected on this page. Pricing, artwork, payment arrangements and delivery details are confirmed before your order moves forward.
